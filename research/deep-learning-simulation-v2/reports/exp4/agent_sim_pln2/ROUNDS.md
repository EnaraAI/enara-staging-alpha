## In short

- **Rounds 1–5:** planner ON was level or ahead in every group except the teens in round 1 (the one check reply does not teach). By round 5 ON led for adults (0.92 vs 1.00) and children under 9 (2.08 vs 2.25).
- **Rounds 6–7:** OFF caught up or moved slightly ahead in adults and children 9–12; children under 9 ended level (1.67).
- **Teens:** the one-step quick check cost one reply; ON caught up in round 2 and both reached 0 by round 4.
- **Children under 9:** the stage moved as designed (listen → do → chant → check), but the lessons had no story, chant or commands to use (the kids pack was not in this test). The 7-day delayed test shows the harm there: 0.792 ON vs 0.896 OFF.
