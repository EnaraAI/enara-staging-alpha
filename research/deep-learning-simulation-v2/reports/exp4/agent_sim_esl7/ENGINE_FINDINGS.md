# ESL7: engine prompt problems reported by tutor agents (simulation only; for the final report, not yet fixed)

## Rounds 5–8 (BASE and NEW unless marked)
1. **"Partly right ... ask why" on fully correct polite requests** (both arms; also in ESL7 round 2).
2. **"Matches the accepted answer: confirm it" on an unchanged copy without a capital or full stop** (s7022, both arms).
3. **"Write one sentence about a person you know using <criterion text>"** pastes success-criterion text that reads badly ("using Finds the final time after a correction"), in many sessions.
4. **NEW: childShort vs the first-time rule block.** A rule, a right example and a wrong example do not fit in 3 sentences of at most 12 words.
5. **NEW: ruleOnWrong wants a right/wrong pair** where none is possible (pure spelling slips, since a misspelled word must never be written; word-meaning points).
6. **NEW: the spacedReview "open with these" line in mid-lesson turns** conflicts with "present the next item".
7. **NEW: the first-time rule block names a rule that is not the learner's actual error** (s7051 word order, s7055, s7058).
8. **The "never ask why" load limit vs "ask what they were thinking"** (likely-cause line), in A1 sessions.
9. **Reading texts above 300 characters are cut off** (amendment D; a product limit).

## Round 9
10. **"Ask for a freer task / free sentence" and "do not ask for another own sentence this session" in the same prompt** (BASE s7054, s7060, s7070, s7035, s7071).
11. **A2 email items cut mid-word by the 300-character cap**, so the date sentence is lost and the item cannot be answered (s7062 m1, s7068 m2; both engine arms). This is the product limit in amendment D.

## Round 11 (session-2 openers)
12. **Important: NEW's "Reviews due: open with a review item, no rule first" (spacedReview) is overridden by the first-time-rule block** ("Present" stage teaching moves). Session 2 restarts at Present, so spaced reviews mostly do not run at the very point they are meant for. In BASE, the "Start here" last-session lines are overridden the same way.
13. **"Finish the open exercise" (last-session line) without saying which exercise it was.**
14. **QUICK CHECK block vs a teaching move that asks for a recall item only** (s7011).
15. **"Use words that appear in no lesson exercise" vs the A1 rule "use only the lesson word list".**
16. **The under-9 sentence cap leaves no room for a full am/is/are rule or for the greeting and reconnect step.**
