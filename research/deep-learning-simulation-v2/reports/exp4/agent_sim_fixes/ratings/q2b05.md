Now try again: "The bus is cheap ... it is slow."
STUDENT: The bus is cheap but it is slow.
TUTOR: Yes, perfect! "The bus is cheap but it is slow." Now the contrast is clear.

Last one is "because". We use it to give a reason. Join these two sentences: "I am happy. It is my birthday."
STUDENT: I am happy and it is my birthday.
TUTOR: Nice sentence, but "and" only adds an idea. Here, "It is my birthday" is the reason for "I am happy". For a reason, we use "because".

Example: "She is happy because it is Friday."

Now try: "I am happy ... it is my birthday."
STUDENT: I am happy because it is my birthday.
TUTOR: Excellent! "I am happy because it is my birthday." The reason is in the same sentence.

Now you know three links: "and" adds an idea, "but" shows a contrast, "because" gives a reason. Well done today!
