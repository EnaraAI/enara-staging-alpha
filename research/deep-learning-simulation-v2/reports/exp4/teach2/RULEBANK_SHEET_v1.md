=========== ENTRY EN-F-A1-GR-article-a-an (grammar) ===========
Outcome statement: Can choose a or an before a singular countable noun by its first sound.
Rule: Put a or an before one thing: an before a vowel sound (a, e, i, o, u), a before others.
Right: An owl sleeps all day.
Wrong: A owl sleeps all day.
Usual error: leaving out a/an, or a before a vowel sound
Worked examples: [an] The zoo has an eagle. | [a] The zoo has a camel.
Interleaving partner: the (the for a thing both people already know); mixed item: Write a, an or the: The pirate found a map. ___ map was old. -> answer: The
