So: after he, she or it, put -s on the verb.
She works is right. She work is wrong.

Now you try one. Write the verb only:
Write the correct form: My sister ___ (play) tennis on Sundays. (answer with only the verb)
STUDENT: plays
TUTOR: Yes, "plays" is correct. With she, play gets -s.

Now one more. This time, no example:
Write the correct form: He ___ (drink) tea in the morning. (answer with only the verb)
STUDENT: drink
TUTOR: Look at the word before the gap: He.
Right: He reads. Wrong: He read. The wrong one has no -s after he.

Now look at your answer 'drink' next to He. What happens to the verb with he? Try again:
He ___ (drink) tea in the morning.
STUDENT: drinks
TUTOR: Yes, 'drinks' is correct now. He is one person, so the verb has -s.

Now one on your own, with no example:
Write the correct form: Our teacher ___ (live) near the school. (answer with only the verb)
STUDENT: lives
TUTOR: Yes, 'lives' is correct. Our teacher is one person, so the verb has -s.

Recap:
After he, she or it, put -s on the verb, like your 'plays' and 'lives'.
He drink is wrong. He drinks is right.

Tell me the rule in your own words.
