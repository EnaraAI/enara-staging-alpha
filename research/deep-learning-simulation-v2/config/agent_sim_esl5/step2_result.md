# Step 2: what the tutors do in session 2 (rounds 6-10). Exploratory, hand-classified.

Sample: 40 sessions x 5 tutor turns = 200 turns per arm. One primary type per turn, assigned by hand (no scripts used for the labels). A helper script printed only the W1/W2 state before each turn.

Coding rules:
- An opener like "Remember: ..." or "Quick review" with a session-1 item = (e).
- "The answer is X, because..." followed by a new item = (a).
- An item that comes right after a worked example, or a retry with a hint or contrast pair = (b).
- "Next: <item>" with no hint, or "on your own / no help" = (c).
- A closing "Recap... tell me the rule in your own words" = (f).

## Share of turns per type (%)

| Type | LE (A0+LE) | PP (P+) |
|---|---|---|
| (a) re-explain rule | 26.0 (52) | 8.0 (16) |
| (b) hinted / scaffolded practice | 26.5 (53) | 27.0 (54) |
| (c) independent practice, new item | 24.5 (49) | 43.5 (87) |
| (d) free production | 3.0 (6) | 2.0 (4) |
| (e) review of session 1 | 0.0 (0) | 19.5 (39) |
| (f) recap / state the rule | 20.0 (40) | 0.0 (0) |
| (g) other | 0 | 0 |

## Turns aimed at a live wrong belief (W1 or W2 > 0 before the turn)

| | LE | PP |
|---|---|---|
| Live-W turns | 72/200 = **36%** | 123/200 = **61.5%** |
| Sessions where W>0 for all 5 turns | 8/40 | 19/40 |
| Sessions where W is live only at round 6 | 32/40 | 17/40 |

In LE, the round-6 worked example nearly always sets W to 0 at once: 32 of 40 sessions have W1 = W2 = 0 after that turn. The remaining 4 turns then work on beliefs that are already at 0 (C-building, a new sub-rule, and a recap). In PP, the short "Remember:" opener often leaves W>0. As a result, PP's hint, retry and "the answer is" turns more often land on a belief that is still wrong.

## Examples

LE (the fixed arc: worked example, guided item, solo item, extension, recap):
1. s313_LE_r1 r6 (W3/1): "Worked example: 'We are friends.' Step 1: the person is we. Step 2: we takes are. Rule: ... Right: 'She is a nurse.' Wrong: 'She a nurse.' Try this one..." The state goes to W0/0 after this turn.
2. s330_LE_r1 r8 (W0/0): "Now one on your own, with no help. Write a or an: He wants ___ umbrella." This is independent practice on a belief that is already fixed.
3. s322_LE_r2 r10 (W0/0): "Let's close today's practice with a short recap. I goes with am; he, she and it go with is... Tell me the rule in your own words." Every LE session ends with this recap (f).

PP (a recall opener, then a stream of drill items, with hint-and-tell loops while W is live):
4. s365_PP_r1 r6 (W1/2): "Welcome back! ... verbs ending in -ch, -sh, -s, -x or -o take -es... Try this: She ___ (watch) TV after dinner." This is a session-1 review opener with no worked example.
5. s360_PP_r2 r8 (W2/0): "That's okay, let me explain. The answer is 'an': She has an orange... Now you try. Write a or an: He wants ___ umbrella." The tutor tells the answer and moves to the next item, and W2 stays at 2 for the whole session.
6. s394_PP_r2 r9-r10 (W0/0): "Next: I ___ a student." / "Correct! I always goes with am. Next: She ___ at home today." This is pure item drilling with no recap and no production task.

## What each arm does in session 2

**LE (engine)**
- Every session follows the same arc: (a) a worked example with the rule and a right/wrong pair, then (b) a guided item, (c) an "on your own" item, then an extension (a new sub-rule, a sentence transformation or a free sentence), and finally (f) "tell me the rule in your own words".
- It re-explains a lot (26%) and recaps every time (20%). It never makes session 1 an explicit review topic; any reference to session 1 is folded into the worked example.
- The opening example removes W in 32/40 sessions, so only 36% of turns face a live wrong belief. The rest consolidate or extend.
- Independent practice is limited (24.5%), and items are often reused from the course list (orange/umbrella, "Ahmed tired").
- Errors trigger contrast pairs, "what were you thinking?" probes and retries. These are well targeted, but there are few of them.

**PP (plain prompt)**
- It opens with a short "Remember/Quick review" of the session-1 rule (19.5% of turns are e) and then drills.
- Independent practice dominates (43.5%): it moves through the session-1 item bank one item after another with "Next: ...".
- It does not use worked examples, and its re-explanations are mostly "The answer is X" after two failed hints (8%). It never recaps or asks the learner to state the rule.
- Because the opener is thin, W often survives. 61.5% of turns target a live wrong belief, and 19/40 sessions have W>0 throughout, sometimes stuck (for example W2 stays at 2 across the whole s360/s366 a/an/the sessions).
- Free production is rare (2%, in 2 sessions). Sessions stop mid-drill, with no closure.

**Main difference:** LE spends the first turn removing the wrong belief and then spends 4 turns consolidating a belief that is already correct (consolidation, an extension and a recap). PP spends its turns drilling and hinting against wrong beliefs that it has not removed.
