## Synthesis
- **LE targets the wrong belief.** In 9 of 10 pairs, every LE R1/R2 event in r6-10 hit W2 while W2 was already 0. W1 (article omission) was never contradicted, so W1 stayed fixed at 1-3.
- **PP's decisive move is one throwaway line about omission.** "We say 'He is a doctor', not 'He is doctor'" logs as R1:W1 and R2:W1. It appears in 5 pairs (s342, s300_r1, s372, s336_r1, s336_r2), usually attached to a doctor item or a given answer.
- **LE responds to blanks by scaffolding and asking for the rule.** It re-prompts the same item, drops to letter-level questions, and ends with "Tell me the rule in your own words." It never gives the answer, so it misses the chance to attach an omission contrast. Even when the learner shows the W1 error ("I saw apple") or states it (s387), LE re-asks a-vs-an.
- **Some pairs are not real PP wins.** In s327 and s384, PP never touches W1 in r6-10. The difference comes from PP's starting state: W2 live in s327, W1 already 0 in s384. In s308 the same pattern shows up for a different belief: LE kept practising forms already at ceiling and never stated the -es/-ies spelling rule.
