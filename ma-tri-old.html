Area of a Triangle: base = 6, height = 4

The area is half of a rectangle with the same base and height. The dotted rectangle helps show why:

  1. Area of a rectangle = base × height:
    6 × 4 = 24
  2. A triangle is half of that rectangle:
    Area = ½ × base × height
  3. Substitute the numbers:
    ½ × 6 × 4 = 12

Area = 12 square units
