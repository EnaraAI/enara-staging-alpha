# LO4 — Learning Results

Simulated learners only. Scores are the average share of held-out items answered correctly, after the LO5 correction. Learning gain counts only learners who did not already know the point before teaching (330 of 360; 30 excluded because they were at ceiling on the pre-test).

| Group | Pre | Post | Transfer | Short delay | Long delay |

## Hint dependency (ENARA group, 330 learners)
Independent mastery 15 | after minimal scaffolding 91 | after extensive scaffolding 15 | correct without transfer 5 | no mastery 204. Average 8 tutor turns and 2.2 hint turns per learner.

## Retention
Of the 121 ENARA learners who mastered the outcome, 81% were still right after the short delay and 59% after the long delay (simulated forgetting, an assumption).

## Taught well and not taught well
- **Taught well** (large gain over no teaching, clearly above static): a/an/some (LO-06), adverb position (LO-04), -s/-es (LO-03), polite requests (LO-05), he/she (LO-10).
- **Not taught well:** irregular past forms (LO-08), questions with did (LO-09), present perfect (LO-11) and finished time (LO-12): final scores 30–45%.
- **Weak profiles:** the persistent-misconception learner (P04) gained almost nothing (17% to 18%); the learner who struggles to express answers (P07) and the careless learner (P05) did worse than with the static material; the guesser (P10) and beginners (P01, P06, P09) gained clearly.
