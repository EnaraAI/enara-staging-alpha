## 5. Adjudication

Each of the 35 disagreements was adjudicated in a separate call. The adjudicator saw the transcript and both evaluations, anonymised as A/B in randomised order, plus the frozen outcome rule. Neither evaluation was presumed right, and scores were never averaged.

- Dispositions: **ACCEPT_INDEPENDENT 21**, **ACCEPT_ORIGINAL 7**, **UNRESOLVED 7**.
- Resulting MEETS count: 70/120 with all unresolved cases as PARTIAL, 77/120 with all unresolved as MEETS, i.e. 58.3%–64.2%.
- **Caveat:** the adjudicator is the same model as the independent reviewer, so a preference for its own reading is possible. Use the dispositions as triage for human review, not as final rulings.
