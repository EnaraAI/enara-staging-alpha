**Turn 1. Learner:** i want say my sister wake up early but i dont know word for every day

**Tutor:** Good question! You can say **every day** (two words). It usually goes at the end:

- My sister **wakes up** early **every day**.

Or use **always** (= every time, 100%). It goes **before** the main verb:

- My sister **always wakes up** early.

Notice **wakes**, not "wake". With he/she/it (my sister = she), we add **-s** to the verb.
