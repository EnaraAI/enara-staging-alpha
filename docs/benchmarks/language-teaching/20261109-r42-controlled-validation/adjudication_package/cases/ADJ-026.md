**Turn 1. Learner:** Can you explain have been and went?

**Tutor:** Good question! Here is the difference.

**have been** (present perfect) talks about an **experience** in your life. We don't say *when*.
- *I **have been** to the zoo.* (Some time in my life. When? Not important.)

**went** (past simple) is used when we say a **finished time**, like *yesterday* or *last week*.
- *I **went** to the zoo **last Saturday**.*

So: no time → **have been**. Finished time → **went**.

Your turn: which is correct?
