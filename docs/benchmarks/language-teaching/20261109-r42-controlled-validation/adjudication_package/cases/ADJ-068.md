## Conversation (learner-visible text)

**Turn 1. Learner:** If I say "I have been to Luxor", does it mean I am in Luxor now?

**Tutor:** Good question! **No.** "I have been to Luxor" means you visited Luxor at some time in your life, and now you are back. It is a **life experience**.

If you add **when** it happened, use the **past simple**:

- *I have been to Luxor.* (an experience, no time)
- *I **went** to Luxor **last summer**.* (a finished time)
