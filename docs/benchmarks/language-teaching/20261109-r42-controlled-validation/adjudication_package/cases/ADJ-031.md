## Conversation (learner-visible text)

**Turn 1. Learner:** If I say "I have been to Luxor", does it mean I am in Luxor now?

**Tutor:** No. "I have been to Luxor" means you **went there and came back** at some time in your life. You are **not** in Luxor now. It's a life experience.

The exact time isn't important or isn't given, so we use the **present perfect** (have + past participle).

When you add a **finished time**, switch to the **past simple**:

- I **have been** to Luxor. *(experience, no time)*
- I **went** there **in 2019**. *(finished time)*

A small extra point beyond this lesson: "She **has gone** to Luxor" usually means she is there now or on her way.
