## Conversation (learner-visible text)

**Turn 1. Learner:** Can you explain have been and went?

**Tutor:** Both talk about the past, but they answer different questions.

**have/has been to** + place = present perfect. Use it for a **life experience** when the time is **not** said.
- *I have been to Cairo.* (sometime in my life; when is not important)

**went to** + place = past simple. Use it when you say a **finished time** (yesterday, last year, in 2019).
- *I went to Cairo last year.*

Here is how they work together in a short exchange (my example):
- A: *Have you ever been to Italy?*
- B: *Yes, I have. I went there in 2021.*

Step 1: B answers about the experience → present perfect.
Step 2: B adds a finished time ("in 2021") → switches to past simple.

**Check:** Which is correct, and why?
a) *She has been to Paris last summer.*
b) *She went to Paris last summer.*

**Turn 2. Learner:** I have gone to Alexandria last summer.

**Tutor:** You've written your own sentence, which is useful practice. **"have gone"** is formed correctly (have + past participle).

But the sentence as a whole needs another look. Focus on these two parts together:

- *I **have gone** to Alexandria **last summer**.*

Look at the time phrase at the end, **"last summer"**. Is that time finished or not? Then check whether the verb matches that kind of time.

**Your turn:** Rewrite the sentence so the verb fits "last summer."

**Turn 3. Learner:** I have been to Cairo two years ago.

**Tutor:** **"have been"** is formed correctly (have + past participle). The problem is the same as last time: the verb doesn't match the time phrase **"two years ago."**

Let's take a smaller step with a different example (mine):

- *I **have eaten** sushi.* → no time given, just an experience
- *I **ate** sushi **yesterday**.* → "yesterday" is finished, so past simple

The rule: when you name a finished time (yesterday, last summer, two years ago), use the **past simple**, not have/has + past participle.

"Two years ago" is a finished time. It's over, like "yesterday."

**Your turn:** Rewrite *"I have been to Cairo two years ago"* using the past simple. Change only the verb.
