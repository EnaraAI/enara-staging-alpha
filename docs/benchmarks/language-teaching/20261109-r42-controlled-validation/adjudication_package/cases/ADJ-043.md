**Turn 1. Learner:** In "This is my sister" — sister is the mother of me?

**Tutor:** Good question! No, **sister** is not your mother.

- **mother** = the woman who has you (your mum)
- **sister** = a girl with the same mother and father as you

Example: Mia and Lena have the same mum and dad. Lena says: "This is my sister. She is ten."

Quick check: a **boy** with the same parents as you is your ___?
