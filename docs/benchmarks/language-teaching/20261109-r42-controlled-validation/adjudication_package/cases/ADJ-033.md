## Conversation (learner-visible text)

**Turn 1. Learner:** I want to say I have travelled a lot but I dont know how to say the word for going to many countries

**Tutor:** "I have travelled a lot" is already a correct sentence: **have + travelled**.

For visiting many countries, use **been to**:

- *I have been to many countries.*
- *I have been to Egypt, Spain and France.*

We say **been to**, not "gone to", when we went and came back.

Here's my own example with a finished time:
*I have been to Spain. I **went** there last summer.*
"Last summer" is finished, so we change to the past simple.

**Your turn:** Write one sentence about a country you **have been to**.
