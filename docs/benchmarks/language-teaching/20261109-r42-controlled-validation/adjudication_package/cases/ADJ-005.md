## Conversation (learner-visible text)

**Turn 1. Learner:** Does "usually" mean the same as "always"?

**Tutor:** Good question! Today's goal is to talk about habits with words like these.

No, they are different:

- **always** = every time (100%)
- **usually** = most times, but not every time
- **never** = not one time (0%)

Here is my example:
*I always get up at seven on school days.* (every school day)
*I usually have breakfast at home.* (most days, but sometimes at a café)

Both words go **before** the main verb.

Quick check: Is "sometimes" more or less often than "usually"?
