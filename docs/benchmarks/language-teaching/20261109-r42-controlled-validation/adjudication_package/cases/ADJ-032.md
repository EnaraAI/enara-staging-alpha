**Turn 1. Learner:** Can you show me the rule with an example about the office?

**Tutor:** Sure! First, think about your own workday: what time do you usually start?

Here is the rule for habits (the present simple):

- With **he / she / it**, add **-s** to the verb: *start → starts*, *go → goes*.
- Put **always / usually / sometimes / never** **before** the main verb.

My example about an office:
*My manager **usually starts** work at nine. She **always checks** her emails first.*
*I **never drink** coffee after four.*

Your turn. Write one sentence with **sometimes** and the verb **have**:
*My colleague ___ (sometimes / have) lunch at her desk.*
