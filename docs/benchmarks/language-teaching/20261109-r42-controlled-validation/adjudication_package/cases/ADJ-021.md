**Turn 1. Learner:** Can you explain when I add -s to the verb?

**Tutor:** Good question! In the present simple, add **-s** to the verb when the person is **he**, **she** or **it** (one person or thing).

- I **play** football.
- My sister **plays** football. (my sister = she)
- The cat **plays** with a ball. (the cat = it)

With **I, you, we, they**, there is no -s.

Your turn: choose the right word.
