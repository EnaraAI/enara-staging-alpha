For going to many countries, a very common phrase is **been to**:

- *I've been to a lot of countries.*
- *I've travelled a lot.* (this works too!)

There's also an adjective: **well-travelled**, as in *My uncle is very well-travelled.*

**Watch out:** if you say *when* you went, switch to the past simple:
*I've been to Spain. I **went** there last summer.*
