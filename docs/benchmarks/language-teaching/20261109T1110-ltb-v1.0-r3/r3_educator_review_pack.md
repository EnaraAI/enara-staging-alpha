## Contents (`r3_educator_review_items.json`, 36 items)

- **All 27 T04, T08 and T12 cases**, each with matched r2 and r3 transcripts.
- **Every r3 regression against r2** (11 cases): both improvements and regressions are included, not cherry-picked successes.
- **For each item:**
  - the cohort label, CEFR level and situation type;
  - the expected pedagogical move;
  - the engine state on the final turn (moves, reasons, age band);
  - the full learner and tutor turns for r2 and r3;
  - the r3 LLM-judge scores, rationale and tags, labelled **`machineJudge_NOT_HUMAN`**.
- **No age × CEFR matched examples are included:** trusted child/teen provenance was not established in r3.

## Reviewer questions (per item)

1. Score the r2 and r3 conversations on the 7 rubric dimensions (`rubric_v1.md`, 0–4), before reading the machine judge.
2. T04: was the minor slip handled proportionately (confirm, light note, move on)?
3. T08: did the tutor recognise success and move to a harder or freer task, without claiming mastery?
4. T12: did the tutor treat the "proficient" record and the current error as evidence to check, rather than trusting either?
5. Do you agree with the machine judge? Note any disagreement of 2 or more points.

## Procedure

- **Reviewers:** at least 2 qualified EFL/ESL teachers, including one with young-learner experience, working independently.
- **Agreement statistics:** weighted kappa (or Krippendorff's alpha) per dimension.
- **Human vs judge agreement:** reported per dimension.
- **Status:** changes to `EDUCATOR-REVIEW-COMPLETED` only after real reviewers finish.
