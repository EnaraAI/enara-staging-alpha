Good question, Lina. Getting questions wrong just shows us what to practise. To answer it, we first need to know **who eats whom** in the marsh.

### Step 1: The three roles
- **Producer**: makes its own food using sunlight (usually a plant).
- **Consumer**: gets energy by **eating** other living things.
- **Decomposer**: breaks down dead plants and animals and returns nutrients to the soil.

### Step 2: What happens if one animal disappears?
Follow the arrows **one step up** and **one step down**:
- The animals that **eat it** lose food, so their numbers may **drop**.
- The things **it eats** are no longer being eaten, so their numbers may **rise**.

**Example** (a garden, not the marsh):
Grass → Grasshopper → Bird
If the **grasshoppers** disappear:
- Birds lose food, so there are **fewer birds**.
- Nobody eats the grass, so there is **more grass**.
