# Note for teachers: new names on your attendance list

*(Draft for Mohamed to send. It goes out only when the attendance fix is switched on.)*

---

**Subject: Your attendance lists now show every student in your class**

Hello,

You may notice more students on your attendance list in ENARA than before. Nothing is wrong. Here's why.

A student can join your class in two ways:
1. **Added to the class directly**, or
2. **Through their group.** For example, every student in "Grade 7B" is in each class taught to 7B.

Until now, the attendance list showed only students added the first way. Students who joined through their group were in your class, saw your lessons and did your assignments, but **did not appear on the attendance list**. We have fixed this.

**What this means for you:**
- Your attendance list now matches your class list.
- The extra names are students who were already in your class. No one has been moved or added.
- Past attendance records are unchanged.

If you see a student who should not be in your class, please contact your school admin.

Thank you,
The ENARA team
