# Curriculum books needed for the assessment engine seeds

For `docs/tutor/ASSESSMENT_ENGINE_PLAN.md` §5. These are the Egyptian national curriculum books, in the Ministry of Education and Technical Education's edition for **2025/2026**. Objectives are taken only from these books, never from memory.

## What I need from each book
- **The cover:** the exact title and the school year printed on it.
- **The contents pages (فهرس):** every unit (وحدة) or chapter (فصل) and every lesson (درس), with their numbers and Arabic titles.
- **If the book has them:** the learning outcomes (نواتج التعلم) or lesson objectives (أهداف الدرس) pages.
- **Not needed:** lesson pages, exercises, answers, or teacher's notes.

A photo or PDF of these pages is enough. Each lesson becomes one objective, with a code like `M4.T1.U2.L3` (Maths, Grade 4, Term 1, Unit 2, Lesson 3). I write the English titles myself.

## Checks before sending
1. **G12 subjects:** the Ministry changed the G12 subject list recently. If the 2025/2026 list differs from the one above (a subject dropped or moved out of the final score), send this year's actual subjects and I'll follow them.
2. **School year:** please use the 2025/2026 books. If you only have an older year, tell me the year so I record it.

## Not needed
- CEFR: already in ENARA, from the outcome bank.
- IGCSE: later.
- Any other grades or subjects.

## Instead of sending the books
Add these sites to this environment's allowed network domains, and I'll read the official books myself: `edu2-egypt.com`, `moe.gov.eg`, `ellibrary.moe.gov.eg`. To do it, open the cloud environment menu in the session's title bar, choose Edit, then Network access.
