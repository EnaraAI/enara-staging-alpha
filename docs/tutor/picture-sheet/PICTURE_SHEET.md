# ENARA picture set: all pictures and their words

21 Nov 2026. Cambridge Pre A1 Starters word list: 495 entries, 494 distinct words. Pictures drawn for ENARA by an agent in the trial style. No known characters, brands or copied designs, and no attribution needed.

![All passed pictures](sheet.png)

## How each picture was checked
- A separate judge agent saw each picture **without the word** and named what it shows.
- A picture passes only if that name is the word, one of its forms, or a fixed UK/US synonym (mum/mother, lorry/truck, rubber/eraser, table tennis/ping pong and similar).
- A failed picture was redrawn, at most 3 tries in total.
