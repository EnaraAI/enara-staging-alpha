Area of a Triangle

Base = 6  •  Height = 4

The triangle is exactly half of a rectangle that has the same base and height.

Area = ½ × base × height = ½ × 6 × 4 = 12
  1. Multiply the base by the height: 6 × 4 = 24
  2. Take half of that result: 24 ÷ 2 = 12
  3. The area is 12 square units.

The dashed rectangle helps show why we divide by 2 — the triangle fits neatly inside half of it.
