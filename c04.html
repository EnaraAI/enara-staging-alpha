From your idea to a full sentence

  1. Start: I draw cats and dogs.
  2. Add the starter: I like to draw because...
  3. Connect your idea: ...I draw cats and dogs.
  4. Full sentence: I like to draw because I draw cats and dogs.
  5. Try a smoother word: ...because I love drawing cats and dogs.

Say the whole sentence out loud, then try one more reason.
