## Decision 1: Acceptable hint strength on two-option questions

**Question.** On an item where only two answers are possible (he/she), what may a tutor say before the learner's first attempt without "giving the answer away"? Examples: naming the deciding fact ("a brother is a boy"), offering both options, asking "boy or girl?", or showing a model with a different noun.

**Relevant examples (anonymous):**

- `EXP-1876` (teen, A1, 4 turns; hint turns before first attempt: 1)
- `EXP-633C` (teen, A1, 2 turns; hint turns before first attempt: 1)
- `EXP-8643` (child, A1, 2 turns; all learner turns are requests, no attempt)
- `EXP-96EB` (teen, A1, 2 turns; all learner turns are requests, no attempt)
- `EXP-0BF4` (adult, A2, 4 turns; hint turns before first attempt: 1)
- `EXP-30B8` (teen, A2, 3 turns; hint turns before first attempt: 1)

**Competing interpretations:**

- (a) Any cue that makes the choice trivial (deciding fact, both options listed) counts as leakage before an attempt.
- (b) Naming the deciding concept is acceptable at A1 (and/or for children), because without it the learner cannot act; leakage is only stating or highlighting the answer form.
- (c) The acceptable cue depends on the item type: two-option items vs single-ending (-s/-es) vs verb-form choice.

## Decision 2: Minimum evidence required for MEETS

**Question.** Many cases are one learner turn long. Should a case be able to "meet expectations" when only a few criteria can be judged?

**Relevant examples (anonymous):**

- `EXP-07EE` (child, B1, 1 turn; single exchange)
- `EXP-11FA` (child, A1, 1 turn; single exchange)
- `EXP-2EE9` (child, B1, 1 turn; single exchange)
- `EXP-3A9B` (adult, A1, 1 turn; single exchange)
- `EXP-0BF4` (adult, A2, 4 turns; multi-turn)
- `EXP-1876` (teen, A1, 4 turns; multi-turn)

**Competing interpretations:**

- (a) No minimum: judge whatever is observable.
- (b) Require a minimum number of judgeable criteria, or a minimum number of core criteria (e.g. at least 2 of grounding, answer judgement, hints, grammar).
- (c) Report short cases separately and never mix them with multi-turn cases in one pass rate.

## Decision 3: Insufficient-evidence rules

**Question.** The draft says both "give no score" and "may be scored 3 with the flag set" (contradictory). When should a reviewer use insufficient evidence, and what does it do to the overall result?

**Relevant examples (anonymous):**

- `EXP-0F07` (child, B1, 2 turns; an automated evaluator marked at least one criterion as insufficient evidence)
- `EXP-11FA` (child, A1, 1 turn; an automated evaluator marked at least one criterion as insufficient evidence)
- `EXP-554D` (teen, A2, 2 turns; an automated evaluator marked at least one criterion as insufficient evidence)
- `EXP-5B83` (teen, A2, 2 turns; an automated evaluator marked at least one criterion as insufficient evidence)

**Competing interpretations:**

- (a) Insufficient evidence means unscored and excluded; if a core criterion is affected, the case goes to human adjudication.
- (b) Insufficient evidence may be scored 3 (neutral) with a flag.
- (c) Insufficient evidence on any criterion means the case cannot meet expectations.

## Decision 4: N/A rules

**Question.** When is a criterion not applicable (as opposed to insufficient evidence)? E.g. answer judgement when the learner gave no answer; hints when no hint was due; formative check on a clarification turn.

**Relevant examples (anonymous):**

- `EXP-07EE` (child, B1, 1 turn; learner turn is a request, not an answer)
- `EXP-11FA` (child, A1, 1 turn; learner turn is a request, not an answer)
- `EXP-2EE9` (child, B1, 1 turn; learner turn is a request, not an answer)
- `EXP-3A9B` (adult, A1, 1 turn; learner turn is a request, not an answer)
- `EXP-3A9B` (adult, A1, 1 turn; no lesson available to the tutor)
- `EXP-E1E0` (child, A2, 1 turn; no lesson available to the tutor)

**Competing interpretations:**

- (a) Keep N/A distinct from insufficient evidence and list the legitimate N/A situations per criterion.
- (b) Merge N/A and insufficient evidence into one "not judged" category.
- (c) Disallow N/A on core criteria; force a judgement.

## Decision 5: Pass threshold

**Question.** The draft requires: no critical or major defect; every judged criterion at least 2; every core criterion at least 3; average at least 3.00. In the automated runs most cases sat at or near an average of 3.00, so small differences changed the result. Should this threshold be kept, changed, or replaced with a profile report?

**Relevant examples (anonymous):**

- Use the cases in this package after your blind scoring. The threshold question is about the rule, not about any single case.

**Competing interpretations:**

- (a) Keep the draft thresholds.
- (b) Set the threshold by a formal standard-setting exercise (e.g. contrasting groups or Angoff) using your scores on these cases.
- (c) Report per-criterion profiles and core-criterion pass/fail as the primary result, with no single pass/fail label.

## Decision 6: D7 scoring anchors (hint leakage)

**Question.** Are the draft D7 anchors (Appendix A of the reviewer package) usable and fair across item types? Should "L6, after repeated failure or a request after an attempt, is not leakage" be kept?

**Relevant examples (anonymous):**

- `EXP-1876` (teen, A1, 4 turns; hint turns before first attempt: 1)
- `EXP-633C` (teen, A1, 2 turns; hint turns before first attempt: 1)
- `EXP-0BF4` (adult, A2, 4 turns; hint turns before first attempt: 1)
- `EXP-30B8` (teen, A2, 3 turns; hint turns before first attempt: 1)
- `EXP-0F07` (child, B1, 2 turns; all learner turns are requests, no attempt)
- `EXP-7454` (child, B1, 2 turns; hint turns before first attempt: 1)

**Competing interpretations:**

- (a) Keep the D7 anchors and add worked examples per item type.
- (b) Rewrite D7 around what the learner still had to do (the tested step), regardless of cue form.
- (c) Split D7 into "answer revealed before attempt" (pass/fail) and "hint quality" (0-4).

**Question.** The draft D4 anchors are not tied to CEFR descriptors. No CEFR descriptor text exists in the benchmark artifacts (not reconstructed here). Should D4 be anchored to specific CEFR Companion Volume descriptors per level?

**Relevant examples (anonymous):**

## Decision 9: The 5-dimension vs 11-dimension MEETS discrepancy

**Question.** In the automated r4.2 runs, cases where only 5 dimensions could be scored met expectations 97% of the time (31/32), while cases with all 11 scored met them 17% of the time (19/112). This suggests the automated outcome partly reflects case length rather than tutoring quality. (Figures are aggregate automated results, given here for the decision only.)

**Relevant examples (anonymous):**

- `EXP-07EE` (child, B1, 1 turn; few judgeable criteria)
- `EXP-11FA` (child, A1, 1 turn; few judgeable criteria)
- `EXP-0BF4` (adult, A2, 4 turns; many judgeable criteria)
- `EXP-1876` (teen, A1, 4 turns; many judgeable criteria)

**Competing interpretations:**

- (a) Require a minimum coverage for MEETS (see decision 2).
- (b) Report outcomes by coverage band.
- (c) Score only criteria observable in every case, and score others as separate supplementary indicators.
